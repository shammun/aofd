# aofd-book — project conventions

## The book in one paragraph per part (our summary)
Vallis, *Atmospheric and Oceanic Fluid Dynamics: Fundamentals and Large-Scale Circulation* (2nd ed., CUP 2017; 22
chapters in four parts, 966 PDF pages; **pdf page = printed page + 20**; notation pages PDF 19–20; references 929–955,
index 956–966; part dividers 21–22, 233–234, 529–530, 749–750).
- **Part I · Fundamentals of GFD (Ch. 1–5).** The equations of motion and thermodynamics of air and seawater (1); rotation,
  spherical coordinates, tangent planes, Boussinesq and anelastic, pressure coordinates, hydrostatic, geostrophic,
  thermal-wind and gradient-wind balance, static stability (2); shallow-water systems, adjustment and APE (3); vorticity,
  circulation and potential vorticity (4); geostrophic scaling, planetary-geostrophic and quasi-geostrophic equations,
  Ekman layers (5). Everything later grows from here; much of it was already taught in Kundu (ch01–ch05, ch13).
- **Part II · Waves, instabilities and turbulence (Ch. 6–13).** Wave fundamentals and Rossby waves (6), gravity and
  internal waves (7), equatorial dynamics and Matsuno–Gill (8), barotropic and baroclinic instability (9),
  wave–mean-flow interaction, EP flux and TEM (10), turbulence (11), geostrophic turbulence and baroclinic eddies (12),
  eddy diffusion and transport (13).
- **Part III · Large-scale atmospheric circulation (Ch. 14–18).** Hadley and Ferrel cells (14), zonally averaged
  midlatitude circulation (15), planetary waves and stationary waves (16), the stratosphere, Brewer–Dobson, QBO and the
  polar vortex (17), water vapour and the tropical atmosphere (18). The most data-heavy part (reanalysis figures).
- **Part IV · Large-scale oceanic circulation (Ch. 19–22).** Wind-driven gyres (19), the upper ocean and thermocline
  (20), the MOC and the ACC (21), equatorial circulation and ENSO (22). State estimates and hydrography (ECCO, WOA23, MIMOC).
- **Chapter 0 · "Start here" (ours, not the book's).** Three parts, each its own notebook and page: **0A maths from
  scratch** (all of Kundu Appendix B item by item + Taylor, complex exponentials, Fourier, ODEs, scaling; PLANNING §15.5),
  **0B climate and GFD basics** (the 17 groups of PLANNING §15.6), **0C reading climate figures** (R4 §3). Informal
  blog-like voice. IDs `C0A-01…`, `C0B-01…`, `C0C-01…`; primers `P0-…`.

No chapter may assume anything Chapter 0 or an earlier chapter (or a K-recap in this chapter) has not explained.
Kundu material is *re-taught* where Vallis first uses it (`cross-book-continuity`), not assumed.

## Two repos, one library
- **This repo `shammun/aofd`**: site, notebooks, explainers, scenes, figure scripts, data registry, movies, and the
  book glue package `aofd/` (parameter sets, notebook helpers, figure wrappers only).
- **`shammun/fluidpy` (`../Fluid Dynamics`)**: all reusable physics (`fluidpy.geo`), data access (`fluidpy.data`) and

## Equation numbering in text
Vallis equations bare: `(2.12)`. Kundu equations prefixed: `(K 13.15)`. Kundu Appendix B: `(B.3.9)`. **Always with the
maths beside the number** — $f\hat{\mathbf z}\times\mathbf u_g = -\nabla p/\rho_0$ (2.xx) — never "by (2.12)" alone.
`tools/coverage_check.py` flags `(K n.m)` vs `(n.m)` mix-ups and a number cited without maths. Every equation is
transcribed from the rendered page image.

## Notation and sign-convention flags (the most common source of bugs)
Record each in `knowledge/notation.md` and `knowledge/symbols.yaml` (with `aka` and ⚠️ `pitfall`), and show a
`> ⚠️ Which convention?` callout at first use.
- **Latitude.** Vallis writes latitude as ϑ (and longitude λ) in spherical coordinates; Kundu uses colatitude θ in
  Appendix B. Check the page image for each chapter; code takes latitude `lat` in radians (`lat_deg` at interfaces).
  Bridge: colatitude = π/2 − latitude, so sin ↔ cos and some signs flip (0A "bridge to Vallis").
- **Streamfunction sign.** State the convention used (u = −∂ψ/∂y, v = ∂ψ/∂x for horizontal ψ; overturning ψ sign per
  chapter and caption). Never assume it carries across chapters, books or datasets.
- **Lapse rate.** Compute with **Kundu Γ = dT/dz** (Γ_a ≈ −9.8 K/km; stable when Γ > Γ_a) and **always show the
  meteorological Γ = −dT/dz** (+9.8 K/km; stable when Γ < Γ_a) alongside — two-row table, conversion, both in legends.
- **Buoyancy b vs density perturbation ρ′.** b = −g ρ′/ρ₀ (Vallis's Boussinesq usage); say which variable a formula
  carries and keep the sign.
- **Rotation sense.** "Clockwise" only with hemisphere and viewpoint; prefer cyclonic/anticyclonic.
- Geopotential Φ vs geopotential height Z = Φ/g₀; pressure in Pa inside code, hPa at the interface (`_hpa`).
- SI everywhere; angles in radians inside functions (`_deg` names at interfaces); nondimensional groups keep Vallis's
  names (Ro, Fr, Bu, Ri, Ek …) and the docstring says which scales define them in that chapter.

## Docstring contract (every library function)
```python
def geostrophic_velocity(dpdx, dpdy, f, rho0=1025.0):
    """Geostrophic velocity from a horizontal pressure gradient.

    Vallis: §2.8, Eq. (2.xx).   Kundu: §13.5, Eq. (K 13.xx) (shared; re-taught as K-recap in ch02).
    Parameters
    ----------
    dpdx, dpdy : pressure gradient [Pa/m].   f : Coriolis parameter [1/s], nonzero.   rho0 : reference density [kg/m^3].
    Returns
    -------
    (u_g, v_g) : geostrophic velocity [m/s].
    Assumptions: Boussinesq, hydrostatic, small Rossby number, f-plane locally.
    Validation: V1 analytic (uniform gradient); V7 sign check in both hemispheres.
    Label: analytic.
    """
```
(Numbers illustrative — always from the rendered page.) `tools/hooks/post_write.py` checks § + eq + units + label on
every file under `fluidpy/geo|data|diag`. Data fetchers add source, licence and citation.

## Validation vocabulary (exact words in reports and `knowledge/concept_map.md`)
`analytic` (V1) · `symbolic` (V2) · `converged` (V3) · `conserved` (V4) · `benchmark` (V5, cited) · `book-value` (V6,
private) · `limit` (V7) · `qualitative` (must appear in Open items) · `unverified` (blocks PASS unless justified).

## Curation tiers (`book.yaml → policy`)
| Tier | ID | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| A · full treatment | `C01…` | 12–18 load-bearing ideas (hard max 18; K rows not counted): picture, question, derivation, worked number, scratch + library code, visualization |
| B · stated | `N01…` | paragraph + equation + number inside the nearest A block; if computable, scratch cell + library + assert |
| C · named | `N…` | one sentence + pointer |
| K · Kundu recap | `K01…` | first Vallis use of a Kundu idea: full re-teaching + deep links; later uses a short recap |
| R · recap | `R01…` | taught in an earlier Vallis chapter (or Ch. 0): short reminder |
| S · skip | `S01…` | exercises, bibliography: one pointer line |
| D · derivation | `D01…` (★–★★★) | written out one move per step |
Chapter 0: the 18-CORE cap and the explainer target apply **per part** (about 12 explainers per part, soft: tools warn,
never fail; the curator says why when above or below).

## What "done" means for a chapter
1. `analysis/chNN.md`, `_curation.md` (tiers for every item, section coverage, 5–14 explainers + backup each with a 3-D
   decision, figure list with dataset keys, 1–3 movie groups, scenes), `_kundu.md` (links resolve), `_design.md`
   (Parts A–G incl. the equation-code table).
2. Library functions exist and are tested in fluidpy; glue in `aofd/`; **full fluidpy suite green** (Kundu included).
3. `tests/test_chNN.py` and `tests/test_figs_chNN.py` pass; `reports/chNN_verification.md` PASS; reviews without open
   Must-fix (`_review.md`, `_code.md`).
4. Explainers and scenes pass lint + shot (+ `--3d`) at 8 sizes with parity rows; `reports/chNN_viz.md` PASS.
5. Data figures reproduced with reading cards and checked numbers; `reports/chNN_figures.md` PASS; registry + credits.
6. Notebook covers every section, every CORE with scratch + library + assert and a visual; `coverage_check.py` clean
   (equation-code ledger, K-recap links, reading cards); executes with 0 errors; lesson + code reviews PASS.
7. `knowledge/` updated; fluidpy tag pushed and pinned; published page, Colab twin, galleries live; `check_public.py`
   and `size_budget.py` OK; `progress.json` all `pass`/`done`.
8. **Movies are optional and async**: a chapter is done without them; `/make-movies N` and `/approve-movie N slug`
   follow, and never block chapter N+1.

## Public-repo rule
Both repos and the media repo are public; Vallis and Kundu are not. Never commit or publish book text, page images,
figure crops or scans, transcribed tables, exercises, or book-quoted numbers (those live in git-ignored
`tests/book_values_chNN.json`). Equations may be shown with their numbers; explanations are ours; figures are
regenerated by our code from open data. `_private/` (figure crops, Kundu App. B, side-by-sides) is never referenced
from a published page.
