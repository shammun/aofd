# BMNG-2004-21600 — Blue Marble NG, September 2004, topography + bathymetry at 2 km; daytime local patches (movie ch00_cross_product_why v4, s23 UV2-14, s31 UV2-25)

## The "pitch dark" map at v2 15:03–15:49 (s23_k_cross): the texture crop, not the lighting
- **What is drawn**: the s23 map is a plane textured with a sub-rectangle of `cp/bmng.jpg`. That file is
  `world.topo.bathy.200409.3x5400x2700.jpg` (sha256 `4c6723e7…` matches the v2 bundle).
  - The crop uses `repeat (0.07, 0.14)` and `offset (0.32, 0.62)`.
  - With three.js `flipY`, it covers **21.6–46.8°N, 64.8–39.6°W**: the open western North Atlantic (Sargasso Sea to
    south of Nova Scotia). It is about 94 % deep ocean.
- **Brightness**: the crop's mean luminance is **15 / 255** with topo+bathy (6 / 255 with topo-only). The whole
  texture averages 63 / 255.
- **Rendering**: the plane uses `meshBasicMaterial` (unlit; ambient and directional lights have no effect) with
  `opacity 0.25`, over the dark movie background. The rendered patch is therefore close to black.
  `outputs/ch00/data/bmng_patches_quicklook.png` (right panel) shows the crop at full opacity, and it is still
  near-black.
- **Globes**: the globes (`GlobeMesh`, `MeshStandardMaterial`, ambient 1.55 + directional 1.35) are lit properly. Their
  dark parts are the deep-ocean colour of the bathymetry texture, not missing light.
- **Verdict**: the cause is the **texture crop (ocean only) plus 25 % opacity**. Lighting plays no part.
- **Fix (for the movie builder)**:
  - Use a land + sea patch at a higher opacity (≥ 0.6–0.8; keep the grid and arrows legible with a dark scrim if
    needed).
  - Or re-centre the existing crop on land.
  - The BMNG texture itself is fine: September 2004 is near the Northern-Hemisphere snow minimum, so no other month is
    needed.

## New fetch and patches
- **Route**: HTTPS (pooch) through `fluidpy.data.fetch` (`nasa_bmng.fetch_image(9, "topography-bathymetry",
  "21600x10800")`).
  - 28,335,770 B in 3.4 s, sha256 `863036b6…e04a2`.
  - It is the same month and variant as the globe texture, at 2 km per pixel (60 px per degree), against 8 km per pixel
    (15 px per degree) in the 5400 file.
- **Why**: a close-up from the 5400 texture is blurry. A 700 km window (s31) is only about 90 px across, and the s23
  crop was 375 px across.
- **Patches** (`nasa_bmng.local_patch`):
  - Each covers lat0 ± h and lon0 ± h / cos(lat0), resampled with Lanczos to a square. A square therefore has equal km
    east–west and north–south at the centre.
  - Each is still an equirectangular lat/lon box, so it can be a globe drape using the sidecar edges.
