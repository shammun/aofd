# CURR-gdp-annual — GDP drifter-derived annual-mean surface velocity (PoC C, Fig. 21.23 twin panel)

- **Route**: AOML ERDDAP griddap `drifter_annualmeans`, subset on the server:
  `https://erddap.aoml.noaa.gov/gdp/erddap/griddap/drifter_annualmeans.nc?U[(-179.875):1:(179.875)][(-72.875):1:(-30.125)],V…,eU…,eV…,N…`.
  The axis order is (longitude, latitude), and ERDDAP serves float64. Estimated size 9.96 MB; actual 9,925,136 bytes
  in 7.4 s. The file is generated by the server, so its sha256 (`cfadb923…c5ba`) is recorded but not pinned.
- **Licence verdict**: the dataset's ERDDAP `license` attribute says **CC-BY 4.0**, with the acknowledgement "This
  study used data collected and made freely available by the NOAA Global Drifter Program
  (https://www.aoml.noaa.gov/phod/gdp/) accessed from https://erddap.aoml.noaa.gov/gdp/erddap/index.html". The
  product page (https://www.aoml.noaa.gov/phod/gdp/mean_velocity.php) asks for a citation of Laurindo et al. (2017).
  R3 listed it as "public domain", which is now corrected. **Redistributable: yes, with attribution.**
- **Derived product**: `data/derived/ch21/fig_21_23_gdp_mean_velocity.nc` (2,841,611 bytes). It holds U, V, eU, eV
  [m s⁻¹] and N, float32 zlib, on (lat 72.875°S–30.125°S, S→N; lon −180 to 180) at 0.25°. U and V are NaN where
  N = 0.
- **Checks**: Drake Passage (66–64°W, 60–55°S) mean U = +0.21 m s⁻¹, so the ACC flows east ✓. The Agulhas box
  (25–35°E, 36–31°S) has mean (U, V) = (−0.17, −0.14) m s⁻¹, pointing southwest ✓. The maximum speed is
  1.34 m s⁻¹. Coverage is sparse south of about 65°S, where sea ice keeps drifters out.
- **Colab route**: the notebook loads the committed derived file and needs no keys.
- **Disk**: before C: 90.8 GB, G: 589.6 GB free; after C: 90.8 GB, G: 589.6 GB; project data on G: 0.09 GB.
